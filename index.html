a message for you

dear sabrina

some things i felt like i needed to say

part 01
our friendship meant a lot to me.

if anything, you were someone i really valued, and i cared about the friendship we had more than i probably knew how to show. i dont think i ever really told you just how much i appreciated having you in my life. and i miss it. i miss the little things, the random conversations, the stupid jokes, the moments that probably didnt mean much at the time but somehow became memories that i still think about. i miss how natural everything used to feel between us. i miss having you as my friend. and i think thats something ive always had a hard time admitting, because sometimes i act like im okay with things changing when deep down, i still look back at what we had and wonder how things couldve been different. you were someone i was genuinely grateful to have. and before anything else, i just want you to know that.

part 02
because somewhere along the way, things started changing.

there were problems, misunderstandings, things left unsaid, and eventually, the distance between us became bigger than either of us probably intended it to be. i dont want to make this about who was right or who was wrong. i dont think our friendship broke because of one single thing. it was more like a bunch of little things slowly piling up until we didnt know how to get back to where we were. and eventually, we became two people who used to know each other so well, but somehow started feeling like strangers. that honestly hurt. because i never really wanted our friendship to end. i wasnt angry at you. i was just overwhelmed with so many things at once, and i didnt know how to handle all of it. there were so many weights on me that i didnt know how to put down, and instead of telling you that i was struggling, i started keeping everything to myself. i know that probably made things worse. i know distance can be confusing when the person creating it never explains why. and im sorry for that.

part 03
there's also something i never really knew how to tell you.

i started liking you in august. and honestly, even writing that now feels weird because i spent so much time trying not to acknowledge it. i didnt know what to do with those feelings. at first, i thought maybe it was nothing. maybe i was just attached, maybe i was just grateful for you, maybe i was just confusing how much i valued our friendship with something else. but the more i tried to ignore it, the harder it became to pretend that i wasnt feeling anything. and i think thats when everything became complicated for me. because you werent just someone i liked. you were my friend. you were someone i genuinely cared about, someone who had already become important to me before i even understood what i was feeling. and thats what made it scary. i didnt want my feelings to ruin something that already meant so much to me. so instead of saying anything, i kept it to myself. i thought silence would make it disappear. it didnt.

part 04
i also want to clarify something, because i dont want you to look back at that version of me and think i had everything figured out.

i didnt. i was confused. it was the first time i had really questioned myself and my own identity like that, and i genuinely didnt know what those feelings meant. i wasnt in the clearest state of mind either. i had a lot going on in my head, and i think i let myself get lost in things that i didnt know how to understand yet. i was basically just trying to figure myself out while also trying to act like i already knew who i was. and i think thats why i reacted the way i did. i actually avoided you because i was confused. i had to go away from you because i thought that if those feelings became bigger, i was gonna drag you down with me. i thought that creating distance would make things easier. i thought maybe if i stayed away long enough, everything would eventually go back to normal. but feelings arent really something you can just switch off. and i dont want you to think that i was avoiding you because i hated you, or because you did something that made me want to leave. it wasnt that. i was just trying to understand myself. and maybe i was a little stupid about the way i handled it. but i was trying.

part 05
looking back now, theres honestly a lot that i wish i did differently.

i wish i didnt become so distant. i wish i communicated instead of disappearing into my own head. i wish i told you when something was bothering me instead of expecting myself to figure everything out alone. i wish i gave you the chance to understand what was happening instead of making the decision for both of us that distance was better. because maybe things wouldve still ended the same way. maybe they wouldnt have. i'll never really know. and thats probably one of the hardest parts. not knowing what couldve happened if i had just been honest sooner. sometimes i think about how different things might have been if i had simply talked to you instead of running away from everything i was feeling. but i cant change that anymore. i cant go back to that version of myself and tell him to stay, to talk, to stop being afraid. all i can do is understand why i did what i did and learn from it. and if theres one thing i learned from all of this, its that silence doesnt always protect people. sometimes silence just leaves people confused. and i wish i knew that sooner.

part 06
so whatever happens after this, i dont really want to force anything.

i dont know where we're supposed to go from here. maybe we'll eventually become friends again. maybe we'll stay as people who once meant a lot to each other. maybe life will take us in completely different directions. and honestly, i dont know. but whatever happens, i just wanted you to know the truth. you mattered to me. our friendship mattered to me. the memories mattered to me. and even the things that hurt mattered, because they were part of something that was real to me. i dont want to erase any of it just because it ended badly. some things dont have to last forever to have been important. some people dont have to stay in your life forever to have changed it. and maybe thats what you were to me. a person who came into my life, stayed through things i didnt think i could get through, became someone i valued more than i knew how to say, and eventually became someone i had to learn how to let go of. i'll always be grateful that you were there. thank you for staying when things were difficult. thank you for being beside me through the bullying and all the shit i was dealing with. thank you for being my friend. and im sorry for the times i made you feel like i didnt care, because i did. i just didnt know how to carry everything i was feeling at the time. i dont expect you to forget anything. i dont expect you to immediately understand. and i dont expect this message to magically fix what happened between us. i just didnt want our story to end with everything i never said still sitting inside my head. maybe this is closure. maybe its just me finally putting into words everything i shouldve said before. either way, im glad i got to know you. im glad i got to call you my friend. and if one day you look back at everything we had, i hope you dont only remember how it ended. i hope you remember that there was a time when our friendship was something i genuinely treasured. because it was. it really was. and no matter what happens from here, thank you for being part of my life.
